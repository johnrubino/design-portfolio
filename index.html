How I Work
01
Understand the System
Before touching a frame, I map the full ecosystem — user needs, business context, technical constraints, and existing patterns. Zooming out first ensures every design decision is grounded in real-world complexity, not assumptions.
02
Research as Foundation
I treat research not as a phase but as a continuous input. Journey maps, usability sessions, and longitudinal synthesis inform every decision and become the source of truth that keeps teams aligned long after launch.
03
Design for Scale, Not Screens
Every component is designed with system thinking. I build with reuse, consistency, and developer ergonomics in mind — so what I deliver doesn't just look good in a prototype, it performs in production.
04
Close the Loop
Post-launch, I measure against the research goals that started the work. Real impact means tracking whether the design actually moved the needle — and iterating when it hasn't. Design is never done; it's just shipped.
